Program 03 · A credit agreement, explained

Replacement vehicle

This is the part the industry is criticised for, so we are blunt about it. Credit hire is a contract between you and us. If the claim fails, or you are found partly at fault, or the charges are not recovered in full, you are personally liable for the hire. We show you the daily rate and that liability in writing, and we will tell you when going through your own insurer would cost you less.

  • Per claim
  • Entitled drivers
  • Credit hire agreement
  • Signed agreement

Program 04 · Independent solicitors

Uninsured losses & injury referral

Policy excess, lost earnings and expenses are recoverable from the party at fault and are routinely forgotten. If you have been injured, that is legal work: we refer you to an independent SRA-regulated firm, we receive no referral fee for it, and you can also claim low-value injury yourself for free through the Official Injury Claim service.

  • Per claim
  • Non-fault drivers
  • No referral fee taken
  • Evidence of losses

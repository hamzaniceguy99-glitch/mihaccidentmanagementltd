Privacy policy

Terms of service

What we provide

MIH Accident Management provides accident management services provided by MIH Accident Management Ltd, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 09869121. Where the company carries on a regulated claims management activity it does so as a firm authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority under firm reference number [FCA FIRM REFERENCE NUMBER], and its permissions can be checked on the Financial Services Register at register.fca.org.uk. The company is not an insurer and not an insurance broker, and arranges no contract of insurance. It is not a firm of solicitors and does not provide legal advice or conduct litigation; any personal injury or legal claim is referred to an independent firm regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, and no referral fee is paid or received in respect of a personal injury claim, such payments being prohibited by the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012. IMPORTANT: where a replacement vehicle is supplied under a credit hire agreement, the hirer enters into a contract with the company and remains personally liable for the full hire charges if the claim is unsuccessful, is found to be partly their fault, or the charges are not recovered in full from the paying party; the agreement, the daily rate and that liability are set out in writing and explained before signature, and no vehicle is supplied without it. Drivers are free to claim directly through their own insurer instead, are entitled to choose their own repairer, and may pursue a low-value injury claim themselves at no cost through the Official Injury Claim service at officialinjuryclaim.org.uk. The company does not make unsolicited marketing calls. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.

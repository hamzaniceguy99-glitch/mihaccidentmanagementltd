Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Is the replacement vehicle really free?

No, and anyone telling you it is should worry you. It is supplied under a credit hire agreement. The charges are normally recovered from the at-fault party's insurer, but if the claim fails or is only partly successful, you owe them.

Should I just use my own insurer?

Sometimes, yes, and we will tell you when. Going through your own insurer may cost you your excess and affect your no-claims discount, but it carries none of the credit hire risk. It is your call and we will set out both.

Do I have to use your repairer?

No. You can use any repairer you choose, including a manufacturer dealer to protect a warranty. An insurer or management company cannot require you to use theirs.

I was injured. What happens?

Injury claims are legal work. We refer you to an independent SRA-regulated solicitor and we take no referral fee — those are banned in personal injury cases. You can also claim low-value injury yourself, free, through the Official Injury Claim service.

Will you cold call me?

No. We do not make unsolicited marketing calls. If someone rings you out of the blue after an accident claiming to be us, it is not us.

What if the other driver denies it?

Then the claim is contested and the risk changes materially — particularly around hire charges. We will tell you that straight away rather than after a vehicle is already on your drive.
